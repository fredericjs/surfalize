# Third-party test files

The files `test_files/sdfio_*.sdf` are interoperability samples from the sdfio project by Thomas Ascher
(https://github.com/aschet/sdfio, `tests/interop_samples`). `sdfio_input_iso2_ascii.sdf` was written by sdfio
(`input_iso2_ascii.sdf`), the `sdfio_mm11_*` files are re-exports of it from Digital Surf MountainsMap 11
(`mm11_iso2_binary_binary64.sdf`, `mm11_iso1_binary_binary64.sdf`). They are redistributed under the MIT license:
